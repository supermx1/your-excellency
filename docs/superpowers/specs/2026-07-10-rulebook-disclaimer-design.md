# Rulebook Disclaimer Design

The rulebook disclaimer should attract attention without continuously competing with the game. Preserve the page's neo-brutalist visual language: thick black border, hard offset shadow, flat yellow attention color, compact uppercase label, and black typography.

Replace the infinite bounce with one short entrance that moves the card into place while its hard shadow settles. The content must remain visible throughout, and the card must be completely static when `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` is active. Add a boxed exclamation mark as a decorative attention cue and expose the disclaimer as a labelled note.

Verification covers Svelte diagnostics, formatting, and the production build.
