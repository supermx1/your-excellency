Run for President across all 36 states + FCT. Build your candidate (billionaire outsider or broke reformer — pick your baggage). Campaign, win or rig an election, govern, survive the tribunal. But the system is built to reward corruption, and your final Legacy score counts your leadership, not your money.

Get rich, or get remembered. You can't do both.
