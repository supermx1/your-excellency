# Brag Plan: Fantasy Naija

## What is this app?
A neobrutalist Nigerian political simulator where you run for President across all 36 states + FCT — campaign, win (or rig) an election, govern, survive the tribunal — in a system engineered to reward corruption, where your final Legacy score counts your leadership, not your money.

## The angle
Everyone has an opinion on how to fix Nigeria. This is the game that calls the bluff. It's a real, playable satire — a clickable map of Nigeria, a character builder that hands you a corrupt-but-rich or clean-but-broke starting hand, and a scoring system where wealth is worth exactly zero. The whole ad drives at one quotable thesis: you can get rich, or you can get remembered — not both.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Bold uppercase line SLAMS onto an amber neobrutalist card: **"EVERYBODY SAYS THEY'D FIX NIGERIA."** Beat. Then, smaller: *"So we built the game where you actually try."* Confident, provocative, universally relatable — earns the next 18 seconds.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The map** — the real SVG map of Nigeria draws in, all 37 states snapping to lime (winning) / rose (losing). This is the "oh, it's an actual game" moment.
- **The character builder** — selects flip from "Broke reformer" to "Billionaire outsider"; the live stat card ticks in: War chest ₦8.0bn · Integrity 70 · EFCC risk 20. You start compromised or start clean.
- **Clean vs dirty** — two neobrutalist action cards: GREEN "Ward-by-ward ground game — builds real support" vs RED "Godfather deal — fast money, no receipts. EFCC go dey watch you." The red one lands like a threat.

## Outro / punchline
Legacy meter reveal — **"Money counts for nothing."** Then the brilliant hook SLAMS the screen: **"GET RICH. OR GET REMEMBERED. — YOU CAN'T DO BOTH."** Cut to the Fantasy Naija wordmark + live URL.

## User flow worth showing
Entry → key action → result, pulled from the real app:
1. **Build your candidate** (setup screen: career/wealth/scandal → live starting stats).
2. **Campaign across the states** (tap the Nigeria map, choose clean vs dirty actions).
3. **Your Legacy is scored** (money contributes 0 — the ending judges your leadership).

## Audio direction
- Role: confident rhythmic bed with attitude — a campaign-ad pulse that carries momentum without turning chaotic.
- Music: upbeat/confident bundled track; beat-synced to the card and stat reveals.
- Music treatment: start immediately under the hook, hold a steady mid-volume presence, land accent hits on the two SLAM lines, resolve with a clean tail under the final wordmark.
- Music cue guidance: bundled track — target strong cues at the opening hook slam (~0.3s), the map fill (~4s), and the closing hook slam (~16.5s); use the beat grid for the stat-row and action-card sequences. Timestamps to be confirmed against the chosen track's cue preset at composition time.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle — let the map's fill and the hero cards' presence breathe slightly with music energy; no waveform bars.
- SFX posture: moderate, motion-matched — impact hit on each SLAM line, soft ticks on the stat counter, a light chime for the green card and a low thud for the red card.
- Audio-coupled moments: stat rows ticking in one by one; green-then-red action cards; the two hard hook lines.
- Restraint rule: no comedy stingers, no cluttered SFX layering — the two SLAM hits must stay clean and land hard.
